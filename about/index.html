The core identity

We stand for the Switnex standard—not a side catalog

As core brand members, we are not here to “resell noise.” In smart-home, features are easy to copy; rigorous compliance thinking and installer-grade intuition are not. DieseRC exists so professional readers can reach Switnex’s technical and narrative source without wading through generic retail pages.

Our mission

Beyond hardware—we engineer confidence

We work with the pain points landlords and asset managers actually hit. The core team frames Switnex around non-intrusive control: wireless, battery-friendly surfaces and documented paths that avoid breaking fixed wiring when used as intended. Our mission is to make compliance a foundation for property value, not a blocker you paper over later.

Three pillars

How the core team shows up

Direct technical guardianship

We stay close to how each product is defined and documented. Internal QA is demanding—including high-cycle endurance regimes for relay-class hardware—because reliability is the moat buyers feel long after the pitch deck.

Mastery of compliance

Market access is existential. We align factory dossiers with SAA / CE / RoHS pathways you can show in a bid pack, and we keep on-page certificate references aligned with official listings. For binding language, always defer to switnex.com and the latest product revision.

Specialist-led support

No scripted bots—people who ship product context. For B2B and volume paths, we route you toward direct, end-to-end conversations: architecture choices, rollouts, and the paperwork that makes projects auditable.

Technical archive & deep docs

Depth lives in static, fast pages—no dashboards, no hidden APIs. Start here when you need manuals, compliance context, or field troubleshooting.

  • Product manuals & model index — searchable on-site, links out to official PDFs where published.
  • DIY & compliance hub — certificates and non-intrusive install framing on one page.
  • Troubleshooting library — symptom-led guides you can share with onsite staff.
Country-specific compliance matrices and whitepaper PDFs are shared when appropriate—request them via the core-team quote or engineer email with your region and project scale.

Certificates at the source

Certificate packs and revisions are curated with factory records and published alongside official product pages—not scraped from third-party marketplaces. Treat switnex.com listings as the live compliance reference.

Direct access to the source

We skip opaque hand-offs. When you reach out through DieseRC or the channels we point to, you are aiming at Switnex core members and specialists—whether you need enterprise pricing, a rollout review, or a documentation deep dive.
