DieseRC SWITNEX

DieseRC designs and produces RF-based remote switching hardware plus connected wall devices across Wi‑Fi, Zigbee and Bluetooth—built for installers who need dependable, low-friction control that stays economical to deploy. In-house manufacturing, application engineering and the SWITNEX product line support projects from access readers and door strikes to lighting, ventilation, signaling and small-footprint DIY retrofits. DieseRC SWITNEX publishes model-level manuals, field notes and compliance-oriented explainers alongside the catalog story.
