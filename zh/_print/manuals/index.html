Product Detail

  • Learning button on receiver – for programming operating modes and resetting
  • Indicator light on receiver – provides visual feedback

Wiring Diagram

  • Input: DC 12V-80V power supply (red: positive, black: negative)
  • Output: To DC motor (red: motor positive, black: motor negative)
  • For winch applications: See specialized winch solenoid relay wiring diagram.

Warning

  • Ensure the power supply voltage matches the receiver’s specified voltage range (DC 12V-80V).
  • Do not exceed the maximum resistive load of 10A (inductive load 5A) for the relay.
  • Disconnect power before performing any wiring or maintenance to prevent electric shock.
  • Keep the device away from water and high humidity environments.
  • Do not attempt to disassemble or modify the device, as this may void the warranty and pose safety risks.
  • This product is intended for use with DC motors only.

Operating Mode

The product is typically pre-programmed in Latched mode. You can change the mode by following the programming steps below.

Momentary Mode

Press and hold the Up (▲) button → motor operates forward
Release the button → motor stops

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button on the receiver 1 time. The indicator light will flash once.
  2. Press the Up (▲) button on the transmitter. The indicator light will flash, confirming successful programming.

Toggle Mode

Press the Up (▲) button once → motor starts (forward)
Press the same button again → motor stops

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button on the receiver 2 times. The indicator light will flash twice.
  2. Press the Up (▲) button on the transmitter. The indicator light will flash, confirming successful programming.

Latched Mode

Press Up (▲) → motor rotates forward
Press Down (▼) → motor rotates reverse
Press Stop (■) → motor stops

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button on the receiver 3 times. The indicator light will flash three times.
  2. Press the Up (▲) button on the transmitter (program forward rotation).
  3. Press the Down (▼) button on the transmitter (program reverse rotation).
  4. Press the Stop (■) button on the transmitter (program stop function). The indicator light will flash, confirming successful programming.

Change Motor Direction (Advanced)

If the motor rotates opposite to the desired direction for Up/Down buttons, press the learning button 7 times. This will swap the output polarity.

Reset

To clear all programmed transmitters and reset the receiver to default state, press the learning button 8 times. The indicator light will flash to confirm. After reset, no remote controls will operate the receiver until they are re-programmed.

Troubleshooting

ProblemPossible CauseSolution
Motor does not respond to remoteNo power to receiver; Transmitter batteries low/dead; Transmitter not programmed; Receiver resetCheck power supply; Replace transmitter batteries; Re-program transmitter (see Operating Mode)
Motor operates in wrong directionIncorrect wiring; Motor polarity reversedVerify wiring (see Wiring Diagram); Use motor direction change function (press learning button 7 times)
Reduced remote control rangeLow transmitter battery; Environmental interference; ObstructionsReplace transmitter batteries; Minimize interference sources; Ensure clear line of sight if possible
Motor runs continuously or does not stopIncorrect operating mode programmed; Faulty transmitter buttonVerify and re-program desired mode; Test with another transmitter if available

Maintenance

  • Keep the receiver and transmitters clean and dry.
  • Regularly check wiring connections for looseness or corrosion.
  • Replace transmitter batteries when the indicator light dims or range decreases. The transmitters use 12V batteries.
  • Do not expose the devices to extreme temperatures or direct sunlight for prolonged periods.

SWITNEX RF 433MHz Wireless Light Switch System User Manual

Product Detail

  • Receiver terminals: Input L / Input N (from power source), Output L / Output N (to appliance)
  • Learning button on receiver – for pairing and resetting
  • Indicator light on receiver – provides visual feedback during programming
  • Antenna on receiver – can be extended for stronger signal

Wiring Diagram

Warning

  • Always disconnect power at the circuit breaker before installing or servicing any electrical components.
  • Installation should be performed by a qualified electrician or in accordance with local electrical codes.
  • Do not exceed the maximum load rating of 10A for the receiver.
  • Ensure all wire connections are secure to prevent electrical hazards.
  • Keep the device away from water and extreme temperatures.
  • The wireless switch requires no wiring – can be mounted with adhesive tape or screws.

Operating Mode

Toggle Mode

Press the button on the wireless switch → light turns ON
Press the same button again → light turns OFF

How to program (pairing):

  1. Ensure the receiver is powered on.
  2. Press the learning button on the receiver 2 times. Wait 3 seconds. The indicator light on the receiver will blink.
  3. Press the button on the wireless switch. Wait 3 seconds. The indicator light will stop blinking, indicating successful pairing.

Multi-Control Scenarios

  • Multiple switches control one lamp: Pair up to 10 wireless switches with a single receiver (3-way switch setup).
  • One switch controls multiple lamps: Pair a single wireless switch with multiple receivers for simultaneous control.

Reset

Press the learning button on the receiver 8 times. Wait 3 seconds. The indicator light will blink rapidly and then turn off, indicating that all paired devices have been cleared.

Troubleshooting

If the receiver and transmitter cannot operate normally, try the following steps:

  • Weak signal reception: Extend the antenna on the receiver. Ensure the switch battery is not depleted. Verify the switch and receiver are within effective range (up to 50 meters in open areas).
  • Switch not responding: Check the 12V 27A battery. Replace if necessary. Re-pair the switch with the receiver (see Operating Mode > How to program).
  • Receiver not toggling: Confirm wiring is correct (Input L/N to power source, Output L/N to appliance). Ensure the load does not exceed 10A.
  • Reset receiver (press learning button 8 times) to clear all pairings, then re-pair.

Maintenance

  • Battery replacement (wireless switch): The 12V 27A battery typically lasts about 5 years. Carefully open the switch casing, remove the old battery, insert a new one with correct polarity, and close securely.
  • Cleaning: Wipe the switch and receiver with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners or immerse in water.

Product Detail

  • NO: Normally open pin
  • COM: Common pin
  • NC: Normally closed pin

Wiring Diagram

Warning

  • Usually the receiver and transmitter have been paired with each other in factory. If not, please reprogram them.
  • Reset before you want to change the operating mode.
  • After reset, all the remote controls cannot work anymore.
  • Replace the battery in time when the remote transmitter voltage is low to get stronger signal transmission.
  • Slightly stretch the antenna on the receiver for better signal reception.
  • Ensure power is disconnected before making any wiring connections.
  • The output type is passive output / potential-free contacts.

Operating Mode

Momentary Mode

Press and hold a button → corresponding relay turns on
Release the button → relay turns off

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button (on the receiver) 1 time. The indicator LED on the receiver will light up.
  2. Press the desired button (e.g., ‘A’) on the remote transmitter. The indicator LED will flash several times and then turn off. Succeed.

Toggle Mode

Press a button → corresponding relay turns on
Press the same button again → relay turns off

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button (on the receiver) 2 times. The indicator LED will light up.
  2. Press the desired button on the remote transmitter. The indicator LED will flash several times and then turn off. Succeed.

Latched Mode

Press button ‘A’ → Relay 1 turns on (Relay 2 turns off if it was on)
Press button ‘B’ → Relay 2 turns on, and Relay 1 turns off at the same time

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button (on the receiver) 3 times. The indicator LED will light up.
  2. Press the desired button on the remote transmitter. The indicator LED will flash several times and then turn off. Succeed.

Mixed Mode

For advanced configurations (e.g., 2 momentary + 2 toggle; 2 momentary + 2 latched; 2 toggle + 2 latched), press the learning button 4, 5, or 6 times respectively, then press the corresponding remote buttons as instructed.

Reset

Press the learning button (on the receiver) 8 times. The indicator LED will flash several times and then turn off. Succeed. (All paired transmitters will be cleared.)

Troubleshooting

If the receiver and transmitter cannot operate normally, try the following steps:

  • Check if battery power is used up. The indicator LED on the remote transmitter does not light up or is dimly lit. Replace with new CR2 batteries.
  • Check if your receiver is wired correctly. Make sure the wiring to NC, COM, NO terminals is correct.
  • Ensure the receiver is within range (up to 50 meters in open areas) and the antenna is not obstructed.
  • Avoid placing the receiver near other strong RF signal sources that may cause interference.
  • Reprogram the receiver and transmitter. Usually this can solve many problems.
  • If the problem still remains, please contact us.
